# Causal Quiet

Causal Quiet is built around restraint after adjustment. The work should make a large first impression contract into something smaller, calmer and less certain. Space carries the argument. The final image must look meticulously crafted, with every mark placed after careful comparison and removal.

Form comes from causal diagrams and effect estimates, but the expression stays abstract. Thin baselines, intervals, small counterfactual marks and measured distance should create the feeling of an observational study being disciplined by its assumptions. The piece needs master-level execution in alignment and proportion, as if it took many rounds to remove everything that did not earn its place.

Color separates temptation from evidence. A warm accent can mark the raw number. A cooler accent can mark the adjusted estimate. The palette should stay narrow, quiet and deliberate, with painstaking attention to contrast and hierarchy.

Typography should behave like a lab label. A few words anchor the interpretation, but the visual system does the work. No explanatory blocks. No decorative statistics. The image should feel like the product of deep expertise: clean enough for a portfolio, precise enough for a methods discussion.

The composition should leave the viewer with one idea: causal inference is often the act of making an exciting number less comfortable. That idea should appear through spacing, scale and uncertainty, not through extra text.
